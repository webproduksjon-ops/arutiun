# Mobile content contracts

Each page has one job on a phone. New sections should not be added unless they answer the page’s assigned question and lead to the next relevant route.

| Page | Primary question | Required content | Primary CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Is this relevant to me? | Positioning, two audience routes, brief practitioner context, first step | Diagnostic meeting |
| Entrepreneurs | Can this help with my leadership context? | Recognizable problems, outcomes, work format, fit boundaries | Diagnostic meeting |
| Individual | Is this relevant to my personal situation? | Common requests, therapy process, expected first step | Diagnostic meeting |
| Approach | How does the work happen? | Methods, boundaries, process, referral conditions | Diagnostic meeting |
| Format | What does it cost and require? | Duration, online format, price, cancellation and first-meeting expectations | Diagnostic meeting |
| About | Who is the practitioner? | Qualification, experience, professional fit, ethics | Diagnostic meeting |
| Materials | What can I learn before contacting? | Topic clusters, short descriptions, one route per article | Relevant service page |
| Article | Does this answer my question? | One search intent, one target query, useful explanation, related service link | Diagnostic meeting |

On mobile, every major section should have one dominant heading, one short supporting explanation, and one next action. Paragraphs should be written for scanning. Detail belongs on the destination page rather than being repeated on the homepage.

Every new article must provide the following front matter: `intent`, `query`, `parent`, and `cta`. The build script rejects an indexable article that omits any of these fields.
